## GPT from scratch

**Repository:** [maximiranda/gpt-desde-cero](https://github.com/maximiranda/gpt-desde-cero)

A video series that builds a GPT step by step in Python, following the GPT-3 architecture at a much smaller scale. Each episode adds one component to the codebase and comes with a notebook that runs in Google Colab, with no local setup required.

| # | Episode | Notebook |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | The tokenizer (byte-level BPE) | [Open in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/maximiranda/gpt-desde-cero/blob/main/notebooks/01_tokenizador.ipynb) |
| 2 | A minimal model that learns (embeddings, softmax, training loop) | Coming soon |
| 3 | Attention | Coming soon |
